My equipment

Enter your equipment precisely: programs and exercises adapt to what you can actually do.

🎯 Detail your gear

Barbells (with their weight), max plate load, dumbbells (min/max), kettlebells, machines (drop-down menu), resistance bands (strength). You can even add photos of your gear.

Guide → My equipment

🔎 Smart filter

Nothing ticked = you see everything. Equipment ticked = only the exercises and programs you can actually do are shown. Everything is editable at any time.

My equipment → Filter
